01 / About me

At the intersection of people & stories.

Phoenix, Arizona
Arizona State University · Walter Cronkite School

I'm Angela, a public relations specialist and storyteller with more than four years of experience bringing people, brands, and ideas into the spotlight.

My background spans media relations, publicity management, campaign development, event promotion, editorial partnerships, and brand storytelling. From Arizona's dining and hospitality scene to retail, music festivals, residential design, nonprofits, and emerging creatives, I love finding the story worth telling—and getting it in front of the right audience.

I began my career at Phoenix-based MMPR Marketing, growing from a digital communications intern into a public relations account manager. Today, I lead public relations for Rodeo Rose Market, Arizona's first all-women's thrift and vendor market, where my work helps amplify women-owned businesses and the community behind them.

I'm also pursuing my master's degree in Mass Communication, with an emphasis in Public Relations, at Arizona State University's Walter Cronkite School of Journalism and Mass Communication, with graduation expected in December 2026. My undergraduate degree is in Journalism and Mass Communication, with an emphasis in Digital Marketing and a minor in Spanish.
